# Illustration content

Terms for the shared content used to illustrate a work across art styles and reader translations.

## Language

**Depicted instant**:
The single coherent visual state chosen for an illustration. A moment is the source span being illustrated and may support more than one depicted instant.

**Canon**:
The fixed identity and appearance of a recurring character, group, place or object. Canon does not establish whether it is present in a particular scene.

## Retired terms

These terms named parts of the full engine's per-moment preparation, which was removed on 9–10 November 2026 ([ADR 0012](docs/adr/0012-one-image-call-from-the-text.md)). The image model now infers place, time, weather, light and situational clothing from the moment's text.

**Section look**:
Shared situational guidance for a story section: place, time, weather, light and clothing.

**Moment conditions**:
The place, time, weather, light and situational clothing resolved for one illustrated instant, shared across its art styles and reader translations.

**Owner look constraints**:
The owner's explicit situational conditions for a section, binding on its moments. Production held none when looks were removed.
