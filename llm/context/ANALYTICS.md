# Client analytics

[src/app/layout.tsx](../../src/app/layout.tsx) mounts Vercel Analytics only for `VERCEL_ENV=preview` or `production`. [analytics.ts](../../src/lib/analytics.ts) defines the typed custom-event contract and tracking functions. Consult it for exact event names/properties rather than maintaining a second schema here.

Payloads use `tier` and `hasCredits` for segmentation, without session IDs or message text. `preference_changed` includes `artStyle`; image generation events (`image_generation_started`, `image_generated`, `generation_error`) carry `artStyle` when known. `feedback_submitted` adds `kind` (`general` or `image`) and, for image reports, the reported image's `artStyle` as the library resolves it. This describes the custom client events, not all stored application data: feedback, billing and operational records have their own fields.

## Interpretation

- Purchase events measure modal opening/closing, invoice creation/copy/cancellation/expiry, and confirmed payment. A modal opens from a purchase CTA (it no longer opens by itself for new visitors), so event-count ratios are not a strict sequential user funnel.
- Generation/navigation/preference events carry a source to distinguish the originating control; verse navigation on the spread reports `keyboard`, `dock`, `swipe`, `mark`, `contents` or `play_on` (Continuous play turning the page at the end of a video; a verse mark reports the verse it opens as `targetUrl`, and `prev` or `next` by which side of the current verse it lies; a Contents tile reports direction `jump`), `contents_opened` records each opening of Contents, and `look_opened` each opening of Look (source `dock` or `translation_name`; Look's changes report `preference_changed` with source `look`, including the new `textSize` preference). A prose moment's page turns report `verse_navigation` with the work's title as `book`; it reports no `verse_view`. Opening the spread's whole-picture view reports `whole_picture_opened` with the verse and the picture's `imageId`; turning pages inside the view reports ordinary `verse_navigation`. Painting on the spread reports `spread_paint` and `spread_retry`, and its automatic-painting switch `spread_paint_switch`. The picture menu reports `picture_menu_opened` (with `pictureCount`, the verse's pictures in the chosen style) on each opening, and its Paint another the generation sources `picture_menu_paint` and `picture_menu_retry`.
- `image_generated.hasCredits` prefers the server-returned balance; most other events use the client balance. Admins can have `hasCredits=false` while still being allowed to generate.
- Ask on the spread reports `ask_opened` (source `words_page` when a question sent from the words page turns it over, `dock` from the phone dock) and `ask_question_sent` (source `suggestion` or `field`, with `messageCount`, the turns including this question). A send the balance cannot cover reports `credits_insufficient` with source `ask_submit`; answer errors report `chat_error_shown` with variant `ask`.
- The map experiment (#532) reports `map_link_shown` once per spread whose words page offers the map, once the link is in view, and `map_opened` when a reader opens it. Both carry the verse, `inherited` (the setting came from an earlier verse) and `debated` (a place in it has more than one proposed site). Exploring the open map reports `map_explored` with the verse `reference` and an `action`: `zoom` (with the `step` chosen: `here`, `land` or `world`), `moment` (a moment link at a place, with `direction` `before`, `after` or `list`) or `all_moments` (opening a place's full list, #556).
- `preference_changed` sources are `look`, `advanced`, `spread_paint_switch` and `translation_selector` (the unavailable-passage page's picker). Generation sources are `spread_paint`, `spread_retry`, `picture_menu_paint`, `picture_menu_retry` and `auto_generate`.
- [analytics-event-utils.ts](../../src/lib/analytics-event-utils.ts) normalizes modal steps, chat errors and post-generation credit state. Preserve deduplication in the calling components when changing effects.

Client events are best effort and do not establish billing truth, model answer quality, or per-user retention. Public API requests use server counters; Nostr selection uses Convex image impressions. See [Observability](OBSERVABILITY.md) and [Nostr](NOSTR.md).

Verification: [analytics tests](../../src/lib/__tests__/analytics.test.ts), [event utility tests](../../src/lib/__tests__/analytics-event-utils.test.ts), and event-specific component tests.
