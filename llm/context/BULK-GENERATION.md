# Bulk image generation

Bulk painting starts from Contents (#446). [ChapterPaint](../../src/components/spread/chapter-paint.tsx) sits above the chapter's wall:

- **The offer.** When the chapter on the wall has verses with no picture in the chosen art style (a tile falling back to another style's picture still counts), one button reads "Paint the N unpainted verses · about X credits". When some of those verses already show another style's picture it names the chosen style instead, "Paint N verses in Watercolor", so it never calls a pictured verse unpainted; such a tile's label names its picture's style. X is the bulk estimate, `estimateBulkCost(N, per-picture catalog estimate)` from [bulk-generation.ts](../../src/lib/bulk-generation.ts), the catalog price read through `useVerseGeneration` with no verse (so nothing is quoted per verse). The owner sees no price. A fully painted chapter shows no button.
- **Starting.** Pressing it starts a job (`scopeType: "chapters"`, label "Unpainted verses of John 1") for exactly those verses, in chapter order, with the reader's model, aspect ratio, resolution, translation and chosen art style. A balance the client check (`canAffordImageGeneration` on the total, as before) says cannot cover it opens the credits dialog and starts nothing (`credits_insufficient`, source `contents_paint`). Pricing that is loading or unavailable disables the button and shows why.
- **Progress on the tiles.** `getVerseStatuses({ sid, bulkGenerationId, verseIds ≤ 200 })` in [bulkGenerations.ts](../../convex/bulkGenerations.ts) returns, per shown verse the job holds, its item status (one `by_bulk_verse` lookup per verse; nothing for another guest). Tiles read "Verse N, queued", "…, painting", "…, painting failed" and "…, just painted" (with a check), and a finished picture appears on its tile from the reactive Contents read, without a reload.
- **The job's controls.** While a job exists (from this chapter or any other) the wall shows it instead of the button: "Painting John 1:7 · 2 of 4" (the earliest verse being painted) with a progress bar, Pause or Resume, and Stop; a finished or stopped job shows how it ended (painted, failed, credits used) until "Done" dismisses it, after which the button offers whatever is still unpainted.

Contents is the only place bulk painting starts, and only for one chapter's unpainted verses. A prose work's Contents offers no chapter painting.

The coordinator below is unchanged.

[BulkGenerationProvider](../../src/context/bulk-generation-context.tsx) sends the job's verses from the browser to `/api/generate-image`, four at a time (#530), eight for the owner (an admin session, #552). A job not yet bound to an engine sends its first verse alone, so every later verse goes out bound. Each verse is claimed queued → generating before it is sent, so no two workers take the same one. Every claim gets a new claim number, and the verse's later writes carry it: Convex refuses a write from a replaced claim, so a requeue still in transit from a tab that lost the run cannot undo the claim of the tab that took over (#552). Painting a chapter takes about a quarter as long as one verse at a time: Genesis 1 about 2 minutes, Psalm 119 about 11. [convex/bulkGenerations.ts](../../convex/bulkGenerations.ts) persists job settings, queue rows and progress so a session can recover its active/paused job. Closing the browser does not leave a server worker running the remaining queue. A job holds at most 8,192 verses (Convex's argument-array limit), and a job is created with the model, aspect ratio and resolution its caller names; a caller that names none (the spread) gets the reader's Advanced image settings, 16:9, 1K and the default model unless changed ([Advanced](ADVANCED.md)).

- Every generated verse uses the normal image route's validation and charging; the total estimate is not an upfront bulk charge.
- Each item is sent as `automatic` generation (#60): nobody accepts a quote per verse, so the server issues and accepts the item's quote itself, requires the whole estimate to be affordable (no spend-down grace), holds the item's maximum and never charges above it ([Sessions and credits](SESSIONS_AND_CREDITS.md#quotes-and-the-maximum-charge)). The panel's total is still the catalog estimate; an item's maximum is twice its own prompt-aware quote.
- Insufficient credits return the item to the queue. When other verses were painting, their holds may be what the balance lacked, so the item is set aside and tried again, alone, once every other verse has settled; refused with nothing else painting, the job pauses (`credits`). The server withdraws a request whose hold it refused, so its id can be sent again ([Image generation](IMAGE-GENERATION.md)). A refusal at the final charge is different: the hold should cover it, but if it does not, the image is discarded and its request recorded failed, so the requeued verse ends failed when it is sent again (409, nothing charged) and the reader paints it again by hand. The first outcome that pauses the job owns its reason and message; a verse still painting that is refused for another reason does not replace them. A verse waiting out a rate limit or a retryable refusal goes back to the queue instead of being sent once the job is stopped, paused or taken over by another tab. The site's rate limit (429 with `retryAfter`) is waited out and the same request sent again, up to five times, before the job pauses (`rate_limited`). The daily spending limit (429 `Daily spending limit exceeded`) pauses it at once (`daily_limit`) and says so. A 503 `Images are temporarily unavailable` (the provider could take no request, #546; the route has already sent a rate-limited or in-flight-refused call again) pauses the job (`images_unavailable`) and requeues the verse with its request id spent: the item's `attempt` goes up and its next request id is `bulk-<job>-<order>-a<attempt>` ([bulkRequestId.ts](../../convex/_helpers/bulkRequestId.ts)), since a retry under the old one replays the failure. Interruption recovery and the release counts read the item's current id. Resume retries a queued item under its stable per-item request ID. Other failed requests are recorded as failed items.
- Any outcome that pauses the job starts no new verses; verses already sent finish and record their outcomes. Pause/cancel controls stop subsequent work; they do not abort an already-sent image request. Stop holds even if an older progress update arrives before it lands. A Resume that arrives while a run is still finishing its sent verses starts a new run once that one ends; a run that ends after its job was dismissed leaves the next job's run alone. A run that ends because a Convex write failed (a claim, an outcome, progress, reconciliation) starts again after 5 s (no recovery write or claim goes out sooner on a new snapshot), up to three times in a row, first settling any verse left generating as after a reload; a verse that paints resets the count. A failed write never marks a painted verse failed, and a write Convex refuses for a replaced claim counts nothing and pauses nothing. An automatic pause holds the job only once Convex has saved it; a pause write that fails is recovered like any other. While the reader's Pause or Stop is on its way nothing is claimed or settled, and a stopped or paused job's failed saves are not retried. Only one settlement of interrupted verses runs at a time, and it alone gives back the run lock it took. A run takes its counters from the verse rows, so a lost progress write is made good, and a job whose verses all have outcomes is completed from them. After the third the job shows as blocked ("Couldn't save progress") and the server job is paused (`save_failed`) if that write gets through. Each pause reason's message comes from one table, [bulk-pause.ts](../../src/lib/bulk-pause.ts), so a reloaded page, and a tab whose job another tab paused, shows the same status and message as the live run.
- **Engine and style binding (#178).** A job records the reader's chosen art style at creation (`artStyleId`) and binds to the engine its first accepted item ran on (`engine`, set through `bindEngine` from the route's response). Every item is submitted with `style` and, once bound, `engine`; the route refuses with 409 "Engine binding changed" rather than admitting the item on another engine, and the coordinator then pauses the job with `pausedReason: engine_unavailable` and keeps its configuration and completed outcomes. A retryable 503 (a scene still preparing, a style temporarily unavailable) is retried three times and then pauses the job the same way (`preparing` or `style_unavailable`). An unbound job paints one verse at a time until one binds it. Resume clears the reason and retries under the same item request id.
- **Recovery.** Items are submitted under the stable id `bulk-<jobId>-<order>` (with `-a<attempt>` once a provider outage has spent one), so a resubmission is a replay, and a replay never spends the session's rate limit. When a browser disappears mid-item, the next coordinator calls `reconcileInterrupted` before submitting anything: it settles items left "generating" from their request records (a succeeded request completes the item with its charged credits, a failed one fails it, an accepted-but-unfinished or never-admitted one goes back to the queue). A 202 from the route (an earlier attempt still running) is waited on, not repeated.
- **Release checks.** `countItems` (server secret) reports, per engine, how many queued or generating items were never admitted (unstarted) versus accepted and not yet ended; an accepted paid attempt is never counted as unstarted.
- Job and verse reads and controls require the verified Convex guest identity: its subject must match the supplied session ID, and the job must belong to that session. Unauthorized reads return no job; writes are rejected. These functions do not take the server secret, so the owning guest can still write item outcomes while the browser runs the queue. See [Security](SECURITY.md) for the distinction from paid generation authorization.
