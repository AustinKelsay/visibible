# Bulk image generation

Bulk painting starts from Contents (#446). [ChapterPaint](../../src/components/spread/chapter-paint.tsx) sits above the chapter's wall:

- **The offer.** When the chapter on the wall has verses with no picture in the chosen art style (a tile falling back to another style's picture still counts), one button reads "Paint the N unpainted verses · about X credits". X is the bulk estimate, `estimateBulkCost(N, per-picture catalog estimate)` from [bulk-generation.ts](../../src/lib/bulk-generation.ts), the catalog price read through `useVerseGeneration` with no verse (so nothing is quoted per verse). The owner sees no price. A fully painted chapter shows no button.
- **Starting.** Pressing it starts a job (`scopeType: "chapters"`, label "Unpainted verses of John 1") for exactly those verses, in chapter order, with the reader's model, aspect ratio, resolution, translation and chosen art style. A balance the client check (`canAffordImageGeneration` on the total, as before) says cannot cover it opens the credits dialog and starts nothing (`credits_insufficient`, source `contents_paint`). Pricing that is loading or unavailable disables the button and shows why.
- **Progress on the tiles.** `getVerseStatuses({ sid, bulkGenerationId, verseIds ≤ 200 })` in [bulkGenerations.ts](../../convex/bulkGenerations.ts) returns, per shown verse the job holds, its item status (one `by_bulk_verse` lookup per verse; nothing for another guest). Tiles read "Verse N, queued", "…, painting", "…, painting failed" and "…, just painted" (with a check), and a finished picture appears on its tile from the reactive Contents read, without a reload.
- **The job's controls.** While a job exists (from this chapter or any other) the wall shows it instead of the button: "Painting John 1:7 · 2 of 4" with a progress bar, Pause or Resume, and Stop; a finished or stopped job shows how it ended (painted, failed, credits used) until "Done" dismisses it, after which the button offers whatever is still unpainted.

Contents is the only place bulk painting starts, and only for one chapter's unpainted verses. A prose work's Contents offers no chapter painting.

The coordinator below is unchanged.

[BulkGenerationProvider](../../src/context/bulk-generation-context.tsx) runs sequential browser requests to `/api/generate-image`. [convex/bulkGenerations.ts](../../convex/bulkGenerations.ts) persists job settings, queue rows and progress so a session can recover its active/paused job. Closing the browser does not leave a server worker running the remaining queue. A job is created with the model, aspect ratio and resolution its caller names; a caller that names none (the spread) gets the reader's Advanced image settings, 16:9, 1K and the default model unless changed ([Advanced](ADVANCED.md)).

- Every generated verse uses the normal image route's validation and charging; the total estimate is not an upfront bulk charge.
- Each item is sent as `automatic` generation (#60): nobody accepts a quote per verse, so the server issues and accepts the item's quote itself, requires the whole estimate to be affordable (no spend-down grace), holds the item's maximum and never charges above it ([Sessions and credits](SESSIONS_AND_CREDITS.md#quotes-and-the-maximum-charge)). The panel's total is still the catalog estimate; an item's maximum is twice its own prompt-aware quote.
- Insufficient credits pause the job and return the current item to the queue. HTTP 429 pauses it with a blocked UI state for manual resume. Resume retries the item under its stable per-item request ID. Other failed requests are recorded as failed items.
- Pause/cancel controls stop subsequent work; they do not abort an already-sent image request.
- **Engine and style binding (#178).** A job records the reader's chosen art style at creation (`artStyleId`) and binds to the engine its first accepted item ran on (`engine`, set through `bindEngine` from the route's response). Every item is submitted with `style` and, once bound, `engine`; the route refuses with 409 "Engine binding changed" rather than admitting the item on another engine, and the coordinator then pauses the job with `pausedReason: engine_unavailable` and keeps its configuration and completed outcomes. A retryable 503 (a scene still preparing, a style temporarily unavailable) is retried three times and then pauses the job the same way (`preparing` or `style_unavailable`); 402 and 429 pause it with `credits` and `rate_limited`. Resume clears the reason and retries under the same item request id.
- **Recovery.** Items are submitted under the stable id `bulk-<jobId>-<order>`, so a resubmission is a replay. When a browser disappears mid-item, the next coordinator calls `reconcileInterrupted` before submitting anything: it settles items left "generating" from their request records (a succeeded request completes the item with its charged credits, a failed one fails it, an accepted-but-unfinished or never-admitted one goes back to the queue). A 202 from the route (an earlier attempt still running) is waited on, not repeated.
- **Release checks.** `countItems` (server secret) reports, per engine, how many queued or generating items were never admitted (unstarted) versus accepted and not yet ended; an accepted paid attempt is never counted as unstarted.
- Job and verse reads and controls require the verified Convex guest identity: its subject must match the supplied session ID, and the job must belong to that session. Unauthorized reads return no job; writes are rejected. These functions do not take the server secret, so the owning guest can still write item outcomes while the browser runs the queue. See [Security](SECURITY.md) for the distinction from paid generation authorization.
