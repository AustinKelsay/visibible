# Chat

[The chat route](../../src/app/api/chat/route.ts) uses the AI SDK OpenAI adapter pointed at OpenRouter, with `streamText()` and a UI message stream response. Ask uses `useChat()` from `@ai-sdk/react` through [usePaidChat](../../src/components/use-paid-chat.ts), which owns the quote, whether the balance covers it, sending with the quote's id and the stale-quote answer; messages use `parts` and may contain metadata.

Each request sends the conversation, the passage reference (book, chapter, verse), the reader's translation and the model. The server does not maintain conversation history.

## Ask on the spread

The verse page asks on its words page ([ask.tsx](../../src/components/spread/ask.tsx), #442). Bible only; the prose reader does not offer Ask.

- **Where.** On a computer the foot of the words page shows "Ask about this verse", two suggested questions and a field. The first is "What does John 1:5 mean?"; from a chapter's second verse onward the second is always "Summarize John 1 so far", and at a first verse it is "What is the historical context of John 1:1?". On a phone that foot is hidden and the dock under the verse holds "Ask a question…", which turns the page over to the same suggestions and field.
- **Turning the page over.** Sending a question, or tapping a suggestion (which sends it), turns the words page over: the `ask` layer ([layers.tsx](../../src/components/spread/layers.tsx)). Its other side shows "Back to the verse", the reference, the verse as a short quote (three lines at most), the conversation with answers streaming in, and a field ("Ask a follow-up…"). On a phone the picture shrinks to give it room. Escape or "Back to the verse" turns it back and focus returns to the control that opened it: the words page's field on a computer, the dock button on a phone. While an answer is still being written, a new question from the words page only turns the page over to it and waits in the field.
- **The conversation** lasts while the reader stays on the verse; follow-ups send the earlier turns as usual. The conversation is keyed by the verse, so a page turn clears it and closes the turned-over page. Nothing is saved.
- **Model.** The default (`DEFAULT_CHAT_MODEL`) unless the reader chose another under Advanced's **Answering model** (`answeringModel` in [advanced-settings.ts](../../src/lib/advanced-settings.ts); [Advanced](ADVANCED.md)); the next quote and question use it.
- **Paying.** Unchanged: the send control shows the quoted price before sending ("3 credits"; its accessible name is "Send question · About 3 credits · at most 13"), the quote's id goes with the question, the server holds and then charges actual usage, and the cap and daily limit apply. With a balance that cannot cover the quote (or no balance, which reads no quote), sending or tapping a suggestion opens the credits dialog and leaves the question in the field; it is not sent after a purchase. A stale quote at a new price shows "Send at this price" on the turned-over page. The owner sees no price and is not charged.
- **Answers** show the notices for an answer cut short or earlier messages left out, but not token counts or latency.

The server builds the context from the chapter so far (#443), so a follow-up or "Summarize John 1 so far" is answered from the chapter up to the verse on screen. The quote is for that context: the client sends the passage and translation with `POST /api/chat/quote`, and asks for a new quote when the verse or translation changes.

## What a request may contain

Ask is the only place to ask. [chat-request.ts](../../src/lib/chat-request.ts) owns what a request may contain and what the model receives:

- **Messages.** Only `user` and `assistant` turns are accepted; a client `system` turn is refused with 400. Parts may be `text`, `reasoning`, `step-start`, `source-url` or `source-document`, and only text reaches the model. Empty turns are left out, and the last turn sent must be the guest's non-empty question (an empty one is refused rather than dropped), at most 4,000 characters ([chat-limits.ts](../../src/lib/chat-limits.ts), also the input's `maxLength`).
- **Rules.** `CHAT_RULES` ends both prompts: stay in role and briefly decline requests outside Scripture and the faith, cite only supplied or certain passages and never invent quotations or word studies, and put safety first, giving helplines only when certain. It is the measured text; change it only with a new eval.
- **System prompt.** `buildSystemPrompt()` is the only system instruction. From the reference it reads the chapter in the requested translation (or, for older clients that name none, the saved translation cookie) (`getChapter` in [Scripture](../../src/lib/scripture.ts), cached like the verse page) and adds the chapter so far: every verse from the first through the current verse, then the next verse of the same chapter (none after a chapter's last verse; never one from the next chapter). The Scripture is held to 3,000 estimated tokens (`CHAT_SCRIPTURE_TOKEN_BUDGET`): a longer chapter loses its earliest verses, the current and next verse are always kept, and the prompt's heading then names the verses left out and tells the model that anything it says about the chapter as a whole covers only the verses it was given. The budget keeps every World English Bible chapter whole except about a dozen read near their end, and Psalm 119. The guidance also says a summary of the chapter so far ends at the current verse. Verse text, captions or a string context sent by the client are ignored or refused. An unknown passage or translation is refused; if the Scripture text cannot be loaded, the prompt keeps the position without the text. For WEB the imported text stands in for the service ([Bible API](BIBLE-API.md#default-translation-imported-text-as-stand-in)). Before #443 the prompt held only the previous, current and next verse.
- **Bounds.** Answers are capped at 2,048 output tokens, or a quarter of a smaller model's context window. History gets up to 6,000 estimated tokens (ASCII characters ÷ 3, plus each other character's UTF-8 bytes, so Chinese counts high rather than low), also limited by the context window after the system prompt and the answer cap; the newest turns that fit are sent and older ones are left out. A turn's quote is priced from its actual system prompt (`chatSystemPrompt()` in [chat-request.ts](../../src/lib/chat-request.ts), shared by the quote and chat routes), so it grows with the chapter so far. A system prompt estimated above 4,000 tokens (`CHAT_SYSTEM_PROMPT_TOKEN_ALLOWANCE`, the Scripture budget plus 1,000 for guidance and rules) is refused ("Passage too long"); with trimming that happens only when the current and next verse alone pass the budget. All refusals happen before credits are reserved.

[chat-models.ts](../../src/lib/chat-models.ts) owns the default, catalog filtering, pricing and fallback behavior. The default is `mistralai/mistral-small-2603`, chosen on the [11 November 2026 comparison](../releases/2026-11-11-chat-model-comparison/README.md); `openai/gpt-oss-120b`, the previous default, stays suggested and keeps emergency pricing. Advanced's answering-model list ([chat-model-selector.tsx](../../src/components/chat-model-selector.tsx)) reads `/api/chat-models`. Do not assume a selected model is currently available simply because it was saved as a preference.

The route may stream for up to 60 seconds (`maxDuration`); an answer still running then is cut off, and its hold is released by the stale-reservation job. The route validates origin, signed session with current-IP tracking, request size, model pricing, rate limit and spending rules. A non-admin request must carry the quote the guest accepted (`quoteId`, from `POST /api/chat/quote`); without a current one the answer is 409 `Quote stale` with a fresh quote, before any hold or model call. The whole maximum must be affordable: a balance below it is answered 402 first, with no fresh quote. A model whose maximum for one message passes 100 credits is refused. The accepted maximum is then reserved. Settlement follows the model's outcome, not stream closure: the final chunk waits for the SDK to finish, and only nonempty output ending with `stop` or `length` is charged, from the reported token usage at the model's prices and never above the accepted maximum; the accepted estimate is charged when no usage is reported, or when either token count is missing, negative or not a finite number. Empty output, error chunks embedded in the stream, provider errors and cancellation release the hold, as does a failed charge. Request abort and response cancellation propagate to the provider. Durable provider-spend receipts and retryable settlement recovery are not implemented. See [Sessions and credits](SESSIONS_AND_CREDITS.md).

Final metadata includes model, token counts, finish reason (`error` for a failed stream), latency and `actualCredits`, plus the actual settlement: `billingStatus` (`charged`, `released`, `unresolved` when the release failed, or `not_required` without a reservation), `creditsCharged` (the final charge or 0), `creditsRefunded` (the whole hold when released, or 0), `estimatedCredits` and `maximumCredits` (the accepted quote), `incomplete` for a charged response cut off by `length`, `maxOutputTokens`, and `earlierMessagesOmitted`, the number of older turns not sent. [chat-metadata.tsx](../../src/components/chat-metadata.tsx) renders tokens, latency, finish reason, and notices when an answer was cut short or earlier messages were not sent.

Ask's send control shows the answering model's quote ([use-paid-chat.ts](../../src/components/use-paid-chat.ts)), reads a new one after each send and when the model, passage, translation or balance changes, and sends its id with the turn. A quote is priced for one model: sending with another model (one chosen in Advanced after the quote, say) is answered `Quote stale` with a fresh quote at that model's price, never charged at the old one. When the server answers `Quote stale`, a fresh quote at the price just accepted (the old one had only expired) is accepted for the guest once and the question is asked again; at any other price Ask shows it and waits for "Send at this price". The owner sees no price. Quotes, holds and settlement are described in [Sessions and credits](SESSIONS_AND_CREDITS.md#quotes-and-the-maximum-charge).

## Changing model behavior

Prompt, model, context, or provider changes must follow [Chat eval and release](../workflow/CHAT_EVAL_AND_RELEASE.md). The spread's Ask is tested through the whole verse page with the quote and the stream faked at the network ([spread-ask.test.tsx](../../src/app/__tests__/spread-ask.test.tsx)), the quote protocol at the routes in [quote.test.ts](../../src/app/api/__tests__/chat/quote.test.ts). Existing [admission tests](../../src/app/api/__tests__/chat/admission.test.ts), [credit-flow tests](../../src/app/api/__tests__/chat/credit-flow.test.ts) and [stream tests](../../src/app/api/__tests__/chat/stream-handling.test.ts) exercise mechanics, not theological quality or safety scoring; `npm run evals:chat` runs the [case set](../../evals/chat/cases.json) for that.
