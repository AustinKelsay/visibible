# Reader and navigation

Verse URLs are `/{book}/{chapter}/{verse}`. [navigation.ts](../../src/lib/navigation.ts) validates locations and computes adjacent verses across chapter/book boundaries, stopping at the ends of the Bible. The root and `/verse/[number]` routes provide redirects.

## The spread

A verse page is the **spread** ([spread.tsx](../../src/components/spread/spread.tsx)), the reader's one screen for one verse, built for the redesign in [#434](https://github.com/AustinKelsay/visibible/issues/434). It is dark whatever the system setting is.

- **Computer (768 px and wider).** The [picture](../../src/components/spread/spread-picture.tsx) covers the window, cropped from its centre. The [words page](../../src/components/spread/words-page.tsx) lies over the right side as dark glass: a line naming the book and chapter, the verse number large, the verse in a serif face, and a foot line with the translation name and the position ("5 / 51"). A floating dock on the picture holds previous, the passage name (it opens Contents) and next.
- **Verse marks** ([verse-marks.tsx](../../src/components/spread/verse-marks.tsx)): a thin row along the top of the picture, one mark per verse of the chapter. A verse with a picture in the stored art style is brighter (`getChapterImageStatus` with the style, the same read the old book menu used), the current verse is white, thicker and `aria-current="page"`, and each mark is a link named "Verse 7, painted" or "Verse 8, unpainted". Marks are not prefetched (the whole chapter is on screen).
- **Phone.** The picture sits above in a square frame (at most about half the screen) and the words page fills the rest. Previous and next sit beside the position line; a dock under the verse holds the Contents button and "Ask a question…".
- **The picture** is the newest saved picture of the verse in the stored art style (`getImageHistory` with the style), unless a version was chosen in the picture menu. Its thumbnail shows first and the full picture replaces it once loaded. While the verse's pictures load the frame is plain dark. Loading the full picture records one impression for it.
- **Whole-picture view** ([spread-picture.tsx](../../src/components/spread/spread-picture.tsx), [whole-picture.tsx](../../src/components/spread/whole-picture.tsx)): the picture is a button named "Show the whole picture of John 1:5". Activating it (pointer, Enter or Space) opens the `whole-picture` layer: the words page slides off to the right on a computer (hidden on a phone, `inert` on both), and the picture is shown uncropped (`object-fit: contain`) on dark, whatever its shape, with the verse under its reference as a caption. On a computer the caption lies over the foot of the picture above the dock, and lets taps through to the picture; the dock and the marks span the whole width. On a phone the picture and caption sit centred above a row with previous, the position and next (the phone dock with Contents and Ask is put aside with the words page). Activating the picture again (now named "Back to the words") or Escape returns to the spread and puts focus on the picture. Previous and next (dock, phone row, arrow keys, swipe, marks) turn the page and the next spread opens in the view too (`useArrivesInWholePicture` in page-turn.tsx, carried with the last turn like the arrival slide); a spread reached any other way opens with its words, and a turned-to verse with no picture gives the words back. Opening it reports `whole_picture_opened`. A verse with no picture offers no view. The dock and the marks fade in this view as on the spread; the caption does not. It replaces the old fullscreen view and lightbox for the Bible reader; [HeroImage](../../src/components/hero-image.tsx) keeps its fullscreen view only for the prose reader until #451.
- **The picture menu** ([picture-menu.tsx](../../src/components/spread/picture-menu.tsx)) is a glass chip at the picture's bottom-left on a painted verse only, reading the shown picture's style and its place among the chosen style's versions ("Classical · 2 of 3"; only the style when it is the sole picture or from another style). It joins the idle fade and sits beside the picture's button, not in it, so its taps never open the whole-picture view; it hides in that view. It opens the `picture` layer (a bottom sheet on a phone, a panel over the chip on a computer): the verse's pictures as thumbnails ([picture-source.ts](../../src/lib/picture-source.ts)), the chosen style newest first, then the other styles the reader may see in the style set's order (`getImageHistory` with `otherStyles`, read only while open; the public style set until Look, #440, supplies the visible styles). The shown one is ringed and focused. Choosing one shows it on the spread ([chosen-picture.tsx](../../src/components/spread/chosen-picture.tsx)) until the page turns; coming back shows the newest. Under the list: **Paint another** ([Image generation](IMAGE-GENERATION.md#painting-on-the-spread)), **Details** (style, date, size and aspect ratio, model, cost in credits) and **Report this picture**, which replaces the menu with the feedback form about the shown picture ([Feedback](FEEDBACK.md)). Escape closes it and focus returns to the chip. Opening it reports `picture_menu_opened`.
- **An unpainted verse** (no picture in the stored style) shows [the unpainted picture area](../../src/components/spread/unpainted-picture.tsx): a blurred glow of the nearest earlier picture of the chapter in that style (`getNearestEarlierPicture`, its thumbnail when it has one), or plain dark when no earlier verse has one, then "No picture for John 1:6 yet", one paint button and the switch "Paint verses automatically as I read". [Image generation](IMAGE-GENERATION.md#painting-on-the-spread) describes the button, painting and the switch.
- **A long verse** scrolls inside the words page; the picture and the foot line stay put.
- **Turning pages** ([page-turn.tsx](../../src/components/spread/page-turn.tsx)): previous and next move one verse across chapter and book boundaries ([navigation.ts](../../src/lib/navigation.ts)). The left and right arrow keys do the same unless the reader is typing in a field ([keyboard helper](../../src/lib/verse-keyboard-navigation.ts)). Each turn reports `verse_navigation` with source `keyboard`, `dock`, `swipe` or `mark`.
- **Swipe** ([swipe-turn.ts](../../src/components/spread/swipe-turn.ts)): a one-finger horizontal swipe anywhere on the spread turns the page (leftward = next). The first 10 px of movement decide: a touch that starts up or down is a scroll and never turns, so a long verse still scrolls; a turn also needs 56 px sideways and more sideways than vertical travel. Two fingers, a touch that starts in a text field, or an open layer other than the whole-picture view turn nothing. Swipe no longer changes picture versions.
- **The next spread is fetched ahead** ([prefetch-next.tsx](../../src/components/spread/prefetch-next.tsx)): `router.prefetch` of the next address, a subscription to the next verse's `getImageHistory` with the same arguments the picture reads (so the next spread finds it already loaded), and `preload` of the picture it will show.
- **Arriving.** A turned page's words slide in from the side it was turned towards and its picture fades up (`data-arrival` on the spread, `data-turn-in` on the animated parts, CSS in globals.css). Arriving any other way (a link, reload, back button) does not animate.
- **Fading controls** ([idle-fade.ts](../../src/components/spread/idle-fade.ts)): on a computer the dock, the marks and the corner name fade after 3 seconds without pointer, wheel, touch, focus or keyboard activity and come back on any. Nothing fades while a layer other than the whole-picture view is open or while focus is inside one of the fading controls. The spread carries `data-idle`; any element marked `data-fades` inside it fades (CSS in globals.css, 768 px and wider only, so nothing fades on a phone). Faded controls stay in the accessibility tree and keep their pointer events.
- **Layers** ([layers.tsx](../../src/components/spread/layers.tsx)): Look, Contents, the corner menu, the picture menu, Ask and the whole-picture view open over the spread, one at a time, never as browser history. Escape closes the open one and focus returns to the control that opened it.
- **Contents** ([contents.tsx](../../src/components/spread/contents.tsx)) is the one way to move around the Bible. It shows the current chapter as a wall: one numbered tile per verse with its picture from a thumbnail ([picture-source.ts](../../src/lib/picture-source.ts)), a count when the verse has more than one picture (in any style, "9+" past nine), a dashed unpainted tile otherwise, and the current verse ringed and focused. The book's name at the top opens the book list with a type-to-filter field (Enter takes the first match); choosing a book shows its first chapter. The chapter row under it switches the wall without leaving Contents. Choosing a tile opens that verse and closes Contents. The arrow keys never turn the page while it is open. Opening it reports `contents_opened`; a tile reports `verse_navigation` with source `contents` and direction `jump`.
- **The Contents read** (`getChapterContents` in [verseImages.ts](../../convex/verseImages.ts)) is one query per chapter given the verse ids and an ordered list of art styles. Per verse it reads the newest few rows to count (at most 10) and the newest picture in each style from the `by_verse_artStyle` index until one is found, so its cost does not grow with the number of saved pictures. The style list comes from [picture-rule.ts](../../src/lib/picture-rule.ts) `pictureStyleOrder`: today only the chosen style, so a verse painted only in another style shows as unpainted; Look (#440) passes the styles the reader may see so tiles fall back like the spread.
- **The corner menu** ([corner-menu.tsx](../../src/components/spread/corner-menu.tsx)): the Visibible name in the top-left corner opens one menu, the `corner` layer. It holds the credit balance (with held credits) and **Get credits**, or "Admin · not charged" for an admin; **Recover credits** (`/recover`); **Send feedback**; **About**; **API documentation**; and the social links ([site-links.tsx](../../src/components/site-links.tsx)). A page opened with `?admin` also shows **Admin sign-in**, which opens the credits dialog at its Admin Access section; without the parameter a visitor sees nothing about admin. **Send feedback** replaces the menu with the feedback form, the `feedback` layer ([Feedback](FEEDBACK.md)). Both are [layers](../../src/components/spread/layers.tsx): one open at a time, Escape or a click outside closes them, and focus returns to the name.
- **The credits dialog** opens only when the reader asks for it (Get credits or Admin sign-in here, and later paint or ask). It is dark like the reader ([Theme](THEME.md)).
- **Reduced motion** makes the slides and fades instant.
- **Not on a verse page:** the old header, tabs, Gallery, verse strip, book menu, details panel, footer, phone navigation bar, chat sidebar and feedback prompt. No dialog opens by itself. `?view=` (including the retired `?view=gallery`) and `?image=` are ignored: a chosen picture version is never part of the address, and the prose reader ignores `?image=` too.
- **The server-rendered page** keeps the verse text, title and description. A passage the translation lacks, or an outage, renders [PassageUnavailable](../../src/components/passage-unavailable.tsx) under the plain site header.

Look arrives with a later ticket of #434.

## The pages around the reader

About, API documentation, recovery, the not-found page and an unavailable passage show the plain [site header](../../src/components/site-header.tsx): the Visibible name and "Back to reading", both linking to `/`. They follow the system's light or dark setting. About, API documentation and not-found keep the old [footer](../../src/components/footer.tsx).

`npm run capture:reader` screenshots each state of the spread at desktop and phone size from a local app ([Capturing the reader](../workflow/READER_CAPTURE.md)). A change that adds a state adds its one-line entry to [states.ts](../../scripts/capture/states.ts).

## The old shell

The library and the prose reader keep the old [header](../../src/components/header.tsx), [footer](../../src/components/footer.tsx) and [LayoutWrapper](../../src/components/layout-wrapper.tsx), which also carries the [chat sidebar](../../src/components/chat-sidebar.tsx) and the feedback prompt, until the prose reader moves to the spread (#451).

[NavigationContext](../../src/context/navigation-context.tsx) owns the book menu, chat/feedback sidebar, header settings menu, fullscreen state, current image ID, and chat context for those pages. Route changes close the book and settings menus; chat and fullscreen state persist. Escape closes fullscreen before chat. Fullscreen locks body scrolling. The [book menu](../../src/components/book-menu.tsx) offers book → chapter → verse selection with image availability indicators.

The [hero image](../../src/components/hero-image.tsx), now used only by the prose reader, shows the selected style's images of a moment, newest first, with newer/older arrows until the prose reader moves onto the spread (#451). When the moment has images in other styles, an "N in other styles" control opens a deliberate other-styles view for that moment and style; changing the style or moment returns to the style's own images, and auto-generation never fires while that view is open. Header generation uses [GenerationContext](../../src/context/generation-context.tsx), with state supplied by HeroImage.

[VideoMode](../../src/components/video-mode.tsx) and [ScriptureDetails](../../src/components/scripture-details.tsx) are not shown anywhere until their tickets (video from the picture menu, passage details in Advanced) bring them back.

## Prose works

`/read` is the library: the Bible, and each prose work in the [catalog](../../src/lib/works/catalog.ts) the viewer may open. `/read/{work}` lists the work's chapters or stories, and `/read/{work}/{moment}` shows one passage. A passage is one moment of the work's active analysis, the same run of paragraphs the image engine draws, and its address is the moment's first unit (`/read/alice-in-wonderland/the-pool-of-tears-1`). [prose-book.ts](../../src/lib/works/prose-book.ts) cuts the imported text (`works/{id}/work.json`) by the active generation's moments, cached per generation for up to a minute on each server instance, so an owner correction to a moment reaches the page within a minute. A work with no active analysis is not found.

- **Who can open a work.** A work is the owner's preview until it is listed in `PUBLIC_PROSE_WORK_IDS` ([proseWorks.ts](../../convex/lib/proseWorks.ts)); the catalog reads that list. The owner here is an admin session ([viewer.ts](../../src/lib/works/viewer.ts)); the configured owner-check session is not enough.
  - For anyone else, an unopened work answers exactly like a work that does not exist: its pages are not found, the library leaves it out, image requests get the same 404, and `/api/image-models?work=` gives the Bible's answer.
  - Convex's image reads by verse id (`getImageHistory`, `getChapterContents`, `getChapterImageStatus`, `getChaptersWithImages`) leave out an unopened work's saved images unless the caller's guest token belongs to an admin session.
  - visibible.com serves only the Bible to readers. Holmes and Alice stay the owner's preview: they verify that the engine and reader work for any work. Other books launch on a separate site built from this codebase. Nothing in public navigation links to `/read`.
- **The passage page** ([prose-reader.tsx](../../src/components/prose-reader.tsx)) shows the image, the passage's paragraphs (Gutenberg's `_underscores_` as italics), and Previous, Contents and Next. The arrow keys and image swipes move between passages across chapters, as they move between verses.
- **Work scope.** The page provides a [work scope](../../src/context/work-scope-context.tsx), and the shared controls read it:
  - The header leads with the passage, its book button opens the contents, and it offers neither translation nor chat.
  - The hero image keeps images under the moment and requests them with the work and unit.
  - The art style controls offer only the work's brief styles. A saved preference the work lacks falls back to the work's first style without being overwritten.
  - Generation is single only, with no bulk.
- **The library and contents pages** have no passage, so their header is the Bible's, and its book button opens the Bible's book menu.
- **Not on prose pages:** the chapter gallery, video, chat context, the book menu and the public image API.

### Serving a work on another site

The generic check closed on 10 November 2026: in production, Holmes and Alice imported, prepared, read and generated through the same engine, image route and reader as the Bible, and stayed hidden from everyone but an admin session. A site built from this codebase to serve other books needs these changes. None of them belongs on visibible.com.

- **Add the work to the build.** Commit its source snapshot under `works/sources/` (listed in `manifest.json`), add an importer to [import.mts](../../scripts/works/import.mts) and commit its approved `works/{id}/brief.json`. `works/*/work.json` is ignored by git and rebuilt by `npm run build`, which runs the importer first, so a work without an importer has no text in a clean deployment. Its unit ids must not collide with another work's, the Bible's included: saved images are keyed by unit id alone.
- **Register and open it.** Add the work and its brief to `PROSE_WORKS` in [catalog.ts](../../src/lib/works/catalog.ts); the reader and image route reject any id that is not there. Then add its id to `PUBLIC_PROSE_WORK_IDS` ([proseWorks.ts](../../convex/lib/proseWorks.ts)). That list lives in Convex, so the change needs a Convex deploy as well as a site deploy; the catalog, image route and Convex image reads all follow it.
- **Link it.** Add a `/read` link to public navigation and sitemap entries for `/read` and each open work, and set the sitemap's `BASE_URL` to the new site's origin. Commit `582872f` (#408, later reverted here by #409) added the link and entries, in [footer.tsx](../../src/components/footer.tsx) and [sitemap.ts](../../src/app/sitemap.ts).
- **Prepare it in that site's Convex.** Each work needs an active analysis, canon and reference sheets there ([work input](WORK-INPUT.md), [work preparation](WORK-PREPARATION.md)). A work with no active analysis is not found.
- **Styles.** A work offers the styles its brief lists that are also in `PUBLIC_GENERIC_STYLE_IDS` ([admission.ts](../../src/lib/engine/admission.ts)).
- **Nostr.** The scheduler skips every image whose work is not the Bible, because a post links to a verse page ([NOSTR.md](NOSTR.md)). A site that posts its images changes that check and the post's link.
- **Known limits.** Alice's court and croquet scenes reached 0–1 of 9 at the launch bar from image-model habits ([record](../releases/2026-11-10-alice-court-fix/README.md)). Holmes reaches 19–24 of 30.
