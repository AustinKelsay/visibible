## Ownership and bounded handling

Source establishes the scene and participants. Selected canon fixes identity and appearance. The moment plan owns place, time, action and effect placement; the section look supplies compatible shared conditions. Engine rules and the work brief constrain every input; art style owns rendering. Rendering `2026-10-24-generic-v7` and review `2026-10-24-rendering-review-v6` share this ownership instruction. The source-first planner and shared-look generator are unchanged.

Every new plan review receives the exact five-field look and complete selected, section-assigned canon stages. Full unselected canon and the planner's evidence/rationale are excluded. Historical isolated review controls may omit the additional rendering input; production preparation always supplies it.

This is a style-neutral content check of inputs to the packet builder, before its optional detail trimming. It does not review a final art-style/reference prompt or guarantee image compliance. It can conservatively refuse a look detail that a particular style's prompt would later trim.

| Report | Handling |
| --- | --- |
| Existing plan faults or `canon_conflict` | One re-plan with feedback; a second rejection stops. Canon conflicts must identify a supplied selected canon entry. |
| `look_conflict` | Stop immediately at the look stage, even alongside plan faults. A shared look cannot be repaired by re-planning. |
| `missing_canon` | Stop immediately at the plan stage, matching `missingCast`. Do not invent a substitute identity. |
| Malformed or unsupported report | Existing single malformed-review retry, then stop unchecked preparation. Look reports must quote a look field; other reports must quote a plan field or selected ID. |

Original looks, canon, source text and owner prose remain intact. There is no automatic shared-look rewrite or per-moment look override. An explicit look correction changes its version and invalidates dependent plans. A later request can pay for another review of an unchanged bad look; this change does not add a persistent rejection cache or improve availability by itself.

The new review marker lazily refreshes old plan approvals while retaining a current look. New problem kinds are additive in Convex; historical records remain readable. The backend schema must reach a deployment before an application writing these audit kinds. No production deployment or activation is included.
