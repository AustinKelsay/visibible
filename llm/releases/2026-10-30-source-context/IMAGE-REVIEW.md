This is a narrow source-state judgment, not blind scoring, human acceptance or an overall image-quality gate. All chosen images are attempt 1. The original JPEGs remain local in the gitignored `evals/images/runs/2026-10-30-source-context/`; committed result and verification files retain their paths and hashes. These observations suggest a narrow house-entry benefit: the two baseline trials with premature growth do not repeat that error in their corresponding candidates. They do not establish a reliable giant-size fix: only one candidate clearly communicates the giant state, another is wrong, and another remains ambiguous. Even changing the ambiguous judgments cannot rescue the predeclared all-three requirement.

The fixed reference **pool** is shared, rather than every actual attachment list being identical. Existing cast selection and priority determine sheet inclusion and order. Gloves repeat 1 and house-entry repeat 2 reverse Alice/Rabbit sheet order; house-entry repeat 1 adds the Rabbit sheet in the candidate. The other pairs use the same ordered assets. Both arms use the identical Alice and style assets whenever selected. This measures the downstream result of the planner-context change through existing assembly, not a renderer-only comparison with equal attachment lists.

The built-in image checker reports no faults on the gloves images because its limited checks do not verify temporary size from the full source. Its clean result is not evidence of source fidelity. All 14 images, 28 check calls and exact provider replies remain accounted. The two lettering retries did not repair the reported fault. No manual rerolls, new reference designs, prompt tuning or production changes followed.
