- **Cast selection:** all three baseline branch replies and two candidate branch replies still describe a cottage without selecting `march_hare_s_house`. The first review had added that ID to the effective draft, but the correction omits it. The other candidate removes the cottage entirely, making omission of its ID reasonable. The retained review's attribution of the cottage to that house is not independently established by the local dialogue. A final semantic review might add the ID back; **this isolated diagnostic did not run that review**, so it does not establish a final production selection regression.
- **Unselected props:** two baseline croquet replies mention flamingo/hedgehog props without their available cast IDs. These omissions also remain unrepaired by a final review here.

Baseline replies turn a vague hollow into a well-like hollow in the tea-party garden, although the source well belongs to the Dormouse's narrated story. Candidate replies keep the vague hollow. This is a possible mixing of the story and the telling, not a proven causal mechanism. Candidate repeat 1 also leaves a speech/timing ambiguity between answering and pausing in thought.
- **Source windows:** the earlier paragraphs explicitly establishing the floating head and legitimate branch remain outside the frozen planner windows. They were used for broader-source inspection, not inserted into the paid requests. Source quotes and other invented incidental details remain available for review.

The draft-aware arm leaves 44 of 84 scene fields literally unchanged; the baseline leaves none unchanged. That mechanical count describes editing, **not semantic fidelity**. Copying a correct detail and copying a mistake both increase it. No scene field required trimming in these replies; normalized corrections retain the same scene text as raw answers.

## Decision

The narrow preservation criterion is **not fully demonstrated** because the smile target is absent in one candidate repetition. This is not a universal failure of including a draft, nor proof that the earlier source-valid instant is wrong. The sample shows useful preservation alongside remaining omissions and inherited problems.

Keep the candidate unapplied. Do not enforce a smile, force every former cast ID back into a scene, or add a work-specific rule to make this test pass. Any next step should first resolve the distinction between preserving an existing draft instant and allowing another source-valid instant, and assess the already-existing final review's role. This run provides no authorization for activation, broader evaluation or additional spending.
